Bihar Land Records Guide

Ownership data (khata / khesra / PID) lives only on official government portals. This app does not scrape or store it — use the links below.

Key terms

  • Khata — account/register number of the landholder.
  • Khesra (Khasra) — unique plot number within a mauza.
  • Mauza — revenue village / survey unit.
  • Jamabandi — record-of-rights showing ownership & rent.
  • Rakba — area of the plot.
  • New PID — Property ID from Bihar's urban property survey.

Check a plot (official)

  1. Open the Bhulekh / Bhumi Jankari portal.
  2. Select District Patna → your Anchal (circle) → Mauza.
  3. Search by Khata, Khesra, or party name.
  4. For old registry deeds, use the Nibandhan portal.

RoR field reference

Fields as they appear in Bihar's Record of Rights (Jamabandi) — compiled from the open ror_bihar_2022 research project:

  • c1 Khata · c3 Khasra — account & plot numbers
  • c5 land type · c6–c8 area (acre / decimal / hectare)
  • c4 plot boundary · c9 nature of possession
  • c10 lagaan (rent) · c14 jamabandi number
  • v1–v9 — ryot name, father's name, residence, thana, district, anchal, village

Disputed land? Verify this way

No public dataset of disputed lands exists. Before buying, verify:

  • 30-year title chain (sale deeds) on the Nibandhan portal.
  • Mutation (dakhil-kharij) completed in the buyer's name.
  • Pending cases in revenue / civil courts for that khata-khesra.
  • Physical possession matches the records.
  • Get a local property lawyer's title opinion. ⚖️

About this project

Open interactive GIS for Patna, Bihar — built with Leaflet, OpenStreetMap and the Overpass API. Optional Google Maps base layers activate when you add an API key in js/config.js.

Highway accessibility analysis

Open research on the Patna corridor (20 × 10.5 km study area): 307 km of mapped roads, 667 highway/arterial segments, 12 major intersections, 280 roadside facilities. Accessibility: 53% high · 44% moderate · 3% low. Toggle the zones, facilities and intersections from the Layers tab.

Data sources
